# Product context: ARPG virtual goods e-commerce

## 1. Project overview

The project is a full replacement of an outdated site with a modern, intuitive, visually strong e-commerce experience for **virtual items** in **ARPG (action role-playing)** games. The goal is to turn visitors into customers through trust and ease of use.

## 2. Market niche and supported games

The platform targets virtual goods in games with deep in-game economies. Supported titles:

- **Path of Exile 1 & 2**
- **Diablo 4**
- **Last Epoch**
- **Hero Siege**
- **Torchlight Infinite**

## 3. Experience pillars (UX / UI)

- **Immersive theme:** **Gamer Dark** aesthetic aligned with the source games—dark palettes with rarity-style accents (gold, purple, blue).
- **Fast navigation:** Structure focused on finding items quickly via **per-game** categories and **attribute-specific** filters.
- **Trust:** A professional interface that avoids an amateur feel, which matters for virtual-item trading.

## 4. Ecosystem scope

The product has three main areas:

### A. Customer-facing storefront

- Dynamic product showcase.
- Advanced filters by item type and game.
- Streamlined purchase flow (cart and checkout).
- Space for social proof and delivery guides.

### B. Admin panel (operations)

- Full control of **stock** and **prices** (real-time adjustments due to volatile in-game economies).
- Order management and **delivery status** tracking.
- Sales dashboard for a high-level view of the business.

### C. Information architecture

- Detailed documentation on **in-game delivery** methods (e.g. direct trade, in-game mail).
- Clear explanations of how the site works for new users.

## 5. End goal

Make item transactions **smooth and professional** so the platform becomes the **first choice** for players who want to save time and progress in the supported games.
